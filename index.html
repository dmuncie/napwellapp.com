Features

🌙

Smart sleep predictions

Wake windows, nap timing, and bedtime predictions based on your baby's actual patterns — not generic schedules.

🍼

Full feeding tracking

Bottle, breastfeeding with per-side timer, and solids. See oz consumed and nursing time at a glance.

👨‍👩‍👧

Whole team sync

Share with your partner, nanny, or grandparents. Everyone sees the same data in real time — no more "did she nap?"

📈

Growth records

Log weight, height, and head size from doctor visits. Track percentiles over time and share with your pediatrician.

✨

AI insights

Weekly analysis of your baby's patterns from a pediatric sleep perspective. Actionable suggestions, not generic advice.

👶

Multi-baby support

Tracking twins or have a new baby? Switch between babies instantly. Each baby's data stays separate and private.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

No setup headaches. No subscription required to get started.

01

Create your family

Sign up and add your baby's name and birthday. You'll get a 6-character invite code to share with other caregivers.

02

Start logging

Tap wake, sleep, or feed. Adjust the time if you forgot to log right away. Takes seconds, not minutes.

03

See predictions

After a few logs, Napwell starts predicting nap windows and bedtime based on your baby's actual patterns.

04

Get insights

Weekly AI analysis surfaces trends you'd miss — like a feeding pattern affecting night sleep, or a growing wake window.
